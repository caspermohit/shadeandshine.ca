## Shade & Shine playbook

- **Hero**: One composition: brand-first name, one line, one CTA, full-bleed car atmosphere — no dashboard clutter.
- **Typography**: Expressive display + refined sans; kill default Inter/Roboto stacks.
- **Motion**: Ship 2–3 intentional motions (hero reveal, package swap, before/after) — not noise.
- **Packages**: Keep escalating PPF visualizer; mirror that clarity on ceramic/tint packages.
- **Proof**: Real project photography + Google reviews near CTAs; before/after as a primary section.
- **Booking**: Primary CTA always visible; quote form pre-selects service from package clicks.
- **Color**: Black studio base + one metallic/blue accent (already on brand) — avoid purple/cream AI clichés.
